The fierce shift

The bones from v3 stay (clean structure, single hero color, native mobile, motion that earns its keep). What changed is the attitude.

1 Black is the canvas

Dark by default. Warm near-black (#08090c) surfaces with hairline #232732 borders. Light mode still works, but the energy lives in the dark.

2 Ember + electric

Intense red (#ff3030) into orange (#ff7a18) for primary actions and accents. Cyan and gold as secondaries. No more pastel.

3 HUD chrome

Corner brackets, scanline overlays, faint grid backdrops, aim reticles, heart-rate waveforms. Borrowed from cockpits and combat displays — used sparingly.

4 Display type that speaks louder

Inter Tight 800 in uppercase for hero copy, with letter-spacing tightened and ember-gradient text on the punchline.

5 Tier system + new logo

Hex-shaped rank badge ("Tier — Ardent") in the hero. The brand mark itself is now a hex in ember gradient — same motif, scaled down. Status that's earned, not given.

6 Copy that doesn't apologize

"Now earn it." "No off days." "Mission complete." "Back to the grind." Words that respect the user's seriousness instead of cushioning it.
